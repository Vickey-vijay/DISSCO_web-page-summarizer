This page provides a history of lighthouses, covering their architectural and technological evolution, the lives of keepers, and their modern preservation.

- Originating from simple hill fires, early structures included the third-century BC Pharos of Alexandria in Egypt, which stood for over a millennium before collapsing in the fourteenth century after long relying on hand-hauled wood or coal fires.
